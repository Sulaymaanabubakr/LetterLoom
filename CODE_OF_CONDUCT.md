# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the LetterLoom community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We welcome contributions that improve the game, documentation, accessibility, testing, and maintainability. We expect participants to communicate with care, assume good faith, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive criticism;
- keeping feedback specific and actionable; and
- showing empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the circumstances. They will respect the reporter's privacy as far as practical.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
